Title: OpenAI kills Sora — surrenders video generation after $1M/day losses
Source: https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/openai-announced-it-would-shut-down-sora-its-once-state-of-the-art-video-model/
Summary: OpenAI will shut down Sora on April 26 (API closes September 24), redirecting the team to world models and robotics. The numbers are brutal: Sora was burning roughly $1M per day, daily active users dropped from 1M to under 500K after the initial mobile launch hype, and Sora 2 Pro sits at #19 on the text-to-video leaderboard. The Disney partnership — a $1B investment tied to Sora — is effectively dead.
Angle: The demo era is over. An impressive AI demo no longer guarantees market leadership — and OpenAI just proved that the hard way with a billion-dollar bet.

Title: GitHub Copilot CLI ships "Rubber Duck" — a second AI model reviews your agent's work before it executes
Source: https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/github-copilot-cli-combines-model-families-for-a-second-opinion/
Summary: Copilot CLI now pairs Claude Sonnet with GPT-5.4 as a review agent called Rubber Duck. It checks plans after drafting and catches errors on complex multi-file tasks. The key stat: Sonnet + Rubber Duck closes 74.7% of the performance gap between Sonnet and Opus alone — meaning a cheap model plus a reviewer nearly matches the expensive model. It activates automatically at critical checkpoints, not on every step.
Angle: The future of AI coding isn't one better model. It's two cheaper models arguing with each other — and that's closer to how real engineering teams already work.
Quote: "To catch different kinds of errors, a different perspective matters."

Title: Anthropic launches Managed Agents — from prototype to production in days
Source: https://www.wired.com/story/anthropic-launches-claude-managed-agents/
Summary: Anthropic shipped Claude Managed Agents in public beta — a suite of APIs that handles sandboxing, tool execution, and deployment infrastructure so teams skip the months of plumbing work. Pricing is standard token rates plus $0.08/session-hour. Early adopters include Notion (team assistants), Rakuten (deployed specialist agents across 5 departments in a week each), and Sentry (bug-to-fix pipeline).
Angle: Building an AI agent used to be an infrastructure problem. Anthropic just made it a product decision instead.

Title: Microsoft ships Agent Framework 1.0 — merges Semantic Kernel and AutoGen into one production SDK
Source: https://techstrong.ai/features/microsoft-ships-agent-framework-1-0-a-production-ready-foundation-for-multi-agent-ai/
Summary: Microsoft unified its two competing agent frameworks (Semantic Kernel for enterprise stability, AutoGen for experimental orchestration) into a single 1.0 release with stable APIs and LTS commitment. Available for .NET and Python, supports MCP and A2A protocols, includes a browser-based DevUI debugger. The "zero to agent in five lines" claim checks out in the code samples.
Angle: Microsoft's agent story was fragmented across two frameworks that confused everyone. Now there's one answer — and it ships with the protocols (MCP, A2A) that actually matter.
